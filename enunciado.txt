Data Mining. Homework 1
The goal of this homework is to study chapter 5 of the book by applying the clustering
and topic discovery strategies explained on a different dataset of HuggingFace.
from datasets import load_dataset
emotions = load_dataset("emotion")
This data set has three subsets:
emotions["train"]
emotions["validation"]
emotions["test"]
and each one, texts and labels. For instance, the first text and its first label is:
emotions["train"]["text"][0]
emotions["train"]["label"][0]
The training text can be used to get some parameters. Then the labels can be used
to report the agreement of the clustering and topics discovered. But the labels are not
the absolute true. You can get better topics!
