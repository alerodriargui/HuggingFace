Data Mining - Homework 1

The goal of this homework is to study Chapter 5 of the book by applying the clustering
and topic discovery strategies to a different HuggingFace dataset.

from datasets import load_dataset
emotions = load_dataset("emotion")

This dataset has three subsets:
emotions["train"]
emotions["validation"]
emotions["test"]

Each subset contains texts and labels. For instance, the first training text and its label are:
emotions["train"]["text"][0]
emotions["train"]["label"][0]

Use the training texts to estimate parameters. Then use the labels to report agreement
with the discovered clusters and topics. However, labels are not absolute truth: you may
find better topics.

Please submit a Jupyter notebook (or similar) in groups of two people, and include
conclusions. You have two weeks to complete it, but start early because there may be
issues with libraries, computational time, etc.
